How organizations change, and who gets left out

I'm Adarsh Raj, a Grade 12 student at Welham Boys' School in Dehradun, applying to study sociology and organizational leadership. I grew up in a business family and became curious about how families, firms and communities organize themselves. My research looks at how family firms pass to the next generation, and at who can afford a home in India's fast-growing Tier-II cities.

Infrastructure, Connectivity and Residential Price Dynamics in India's Tier-II Cities

A comparative study of how new infrastructure lines up with housing prices in five cities, with Siliguri, a gateway to the North-East, as a qualitative case. It keeps a hard line between association and causation.
